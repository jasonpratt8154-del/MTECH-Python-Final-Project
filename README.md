# MTECH-Python-Final-Project
## Introduction
The data set used is the census income data from the UC Irvine Machine Learning Repository.
## Data Manipulation
The data was grouped by income and education to make it easy to extract the high income individuals and high education individuals from the low income individuals and low education individuals and make it easier to see differences.

## Analysis

Based on the data and the visualizations, we can see that education number has the greatest effect on income by a significant amount, which makes sense as it is harder to get jobs that require higher education levels and thus the jobs are higher paying. 
Age had a normal bell curve in relation to income, with ages 40-50 making the most money, and those older and younger making less, likely due to retirement. It is important to note that the taper off for older ages doesn't happen at retirement, but slightly younger.
All college degrees saw a significant increase in high earners, with another increase for graduate school.
